Before We Begin...

This app can't buy real tickets, hold real money, or predict anything. It just makes picking numbers more fun.

BUT — if you take a line generated here, buy an actual ticket with it, and it genuinely wins something life-changing... by tapping below you hereby pledge, on your honour as a good sport, to shout the developer a coffee ☕ (or a modest, entirely voluntary slice of the winnings 😇) as a thank-you.

No legal obligation. No fine print that matters. Just vibes and good karma.
